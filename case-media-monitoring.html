Media Monitoring

Know you were mentioned before someone forwards you the clip.

What it is

A media monitoring dashboard that tracks mentions across outlets and surfaces them in one place. Built alongside our public safety work, where knowing what is being reported is an operational requirement rather than a PR nicety.

PHPData pipelinesMySQLDashboards

The problem

Mentions are scattered across print, broadcast and online outlets, and the organizations that most need to know about them find out last, usually from someone forwarding a link hours after publication.

What we shipped

  • Mention tracking across multiple outlet types
  • A single dashboard aggregating all monitored sources
  • Filtering and search across captured coverage
  • Alerting on new coverage as it appears

Where it stands today

  • Coverage arrives in one dashboard instead of several inboxes
  • Search works across the historical archive, not just today
  • Built as part of the Safe City systems family
  • Deployed in a government and enterprise context
