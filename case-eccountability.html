Eccountability

Mastermind groups that keep meeting after week three.

What it is

A platform for mastermind and accountability groups: video sessions, goal tracking and a directory of tribes. The product problem is attendance decay, so everything is built to make the next session easy to show up to.

Node.jsVue.jsWebRTCMongoDB

The problem

Accountability groups start strong and dissolve quietly. Sessions get scheduled in one tool, goals are tracked in another, and the group directory is a spreadsheet, so the friction of continuing exceeds the friction of stopping.

What we shipped

  • Video sessions inside the platform
  • Goal tracking visible to the whole group
  • A tribes directory for discovering and joining groups
  • Session scheduling tied to the group, not an individual

Where it stands today

  • Sessions, goals and membership live in one place
  • Commitments are visible to the people holding you to them
  • New members find groups through the directory
  • The friction of showing up is lower than the friction of quitting

Click a stage

Designed against attendance decay.

Find a tribe

Commit

Track
