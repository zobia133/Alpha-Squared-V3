RheumaTak

Arthritis management that survives a bad-hands day.

What it is

An app for arthritis patients to manage medication schedules and track disease activity. The interface constraint is unusual and non-negotiable: it has to be usable by someone whose hands hurt.

SwiftKotlinPHP / LaravelNotifications

The problem

Rheumatology depends on what happened between appointments, and patients are expected to recall months of symptom fluctuation from memory. Paper diaries get abandoned, and typing-heavy apps get abandoned faster by exactly the population that needs them.

What we shipped

  • Medication schedules with adherence reminders
  • Disease activity tracking with low-effort input
  • Trend views a clinician can read in a consultation
  • Large targets and minimal typing throughout

Where it stands today

  • Patients arrive at appointments with data instead of recollection
  • Adherence reminders are scheduled around the regimen, not generic
  • Input effort was treated as a clinical requirement
  • Native mobile on iOS and Android
