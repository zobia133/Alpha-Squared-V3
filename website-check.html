What we check

Six services, six scores.

Every check maps to work we actually do, so each low score comes with a concrete way to fix it.

01 · Operations Platforms

HTTPS, mixed content and security headers (CSP, HSTS, clickjacking).

02 · Mobile Apps

Viewport, pinch-zoom, responsive images, tap-to-call and companion apps.

03 · AI & Automation

Chat or voice assistant, online booking, CRM automation, lead forms and analytics.

04 · Product & UX Design

Titles, descriptions, headings, alt text, share previews and search-engine files.

05 · Dedicated Teams

Code upkeep: outdated jQuery or WordPress, script bloat and legacy markup.

06 · Cloud, DevOps & Support

Server response time, compression, caching, CDN and render-blocking scripts.
