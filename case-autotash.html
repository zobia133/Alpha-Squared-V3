Autotash

One part request, every supplier in the kingdom bidding.

What it is

A spare-parts marketplace for the Saudi automotive industry. A buyer describes the part once and suppliers come back with quotes, which inverts a process that traditionally means calling twelve shops in sequence.

PHP / LaravelVue.jsSwiftKotlin

The problem

Sourcing a specific automotive part meant phoning suppliers one at a time, describing the same vehicle and part repeatedly, and having no way to compare prices except memory. Suppliers, meanwhile, only heard about demand they happened to be phoned about.

What we shipped

  • Structured part requests by vehicle, year and component
  • Broadcast of a single request to matching suppliers
  • Quote comparison side by side for the buyer
  • Supplier dashboard for inbound requests and quote management

Where it stands today

  • One request reaches every relevant supplier at once
  • Buyers compare quotes instead of remembering them
  • Suppliers see demand they would never have been called about
  • Built for the KSA automotive market

Click a stage

Twelve phone calls, collapsed into one request.

Describe

Vehicle, year and component are captured as structured fields, so suppliers are all quoting the same thing.

Broadcast

The request goes out to every supplier who stocks that category, not just the ones the buyer knows to call.

Quote

Suppliers respond with price and availability from their dashboard. Competing quotes arrive in parallel.

Compare

The buyer sees quotes side by side, with supplier history attached. Price is not the only visible variable.

Order

The buyer accepts a quote and the supplier gets a confirmed order rather than a maybe.
