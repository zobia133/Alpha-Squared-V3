For You Deals Only: a deals brand that had to look like one.

What it is

A deals and offers marketing website. Short build, clear brief: make the offer the hero on every screen size and get out of the way of the click.

PHPWordPressResponsiveSEO

The problem

Deals sites live and die on scannability. If a visitor cannot assess an offer in a second and a half, they leave, and every design flourish that delays that assessment costs conversions.

What we shipped

  • Offer-first layout across all breakpoints
  • Category browsing for deal types
  • Fast-loading pages with minimal render blocking
  • Clear paths from offer to action

Where it stands today

  • The offer is the largest thing on every screen
  • Page weight kept deliberately low
  • Categories let repeat visitors skip to what they want
  • Responsive from phone to desktop

The brief

Everything serves the scan.
