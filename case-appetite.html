App'etite

Order dinner, fund a meal for someone else.

What it is

A food app that attaches a charitable contribution to the ordering experience. The pitch is simple: eat great food while contributing to causes, without turning the checkout into a guilt trip.

SwiftKotlinPHP / LaravelPayments

The problem

Cause-linked commerce usually fails in one of two ways. Either the charity element is buried where nobody sees it, or it is so prominent that ordering food feels like a fundraising appeal.

What we shipped

  • Standard food ordering and discovery flows
  • Contribution attached to the order, visible but not intrusive
  • Cause selection so the user directs the impact
  • Cumulative impact tracking per user

Where it stands today

  • The charitable element is present at checkout without hijacking it
  • Users can see their cumulative contribution over time
  • Restaurants participate without changing their own operations
  • Native mobile app
