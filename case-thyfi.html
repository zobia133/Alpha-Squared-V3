ThyFi

Reorder in two taps, because most orders are repeat orders.

What it is

A food ordering app built around the observation that most people order the same handful of things. Categories and discovery matter, but favorites and order history are what actually get used.

SwiftKotlinNode.jsPayments

The problem

Food ordering apps optimize hard for first-time discovery and then make the twentieth order take just as long as the first. That is backwards: the repeat order is the whole business.

What we shipped

  • Category browsing and restaurant discovery
  • Favorites for the items people actually reorder
  • Order history with fast reorder
  • Profile, addresses and payment preferences

Where it stands today

  • Reorder is the shortest path in the app, not the longest
  • Favorites carry across sessions and devices
  • Discovery is still there for the times it matters
  • Native on iOS and Android

Design priority

Optimized for order twenty, not order one.
