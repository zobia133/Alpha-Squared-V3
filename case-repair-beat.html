Repair Beat

A repair shop's entire day, from intake ticket to paid invoice.

What it is

A cloud-based POS and operations platform for mobile and electronics repair businesses. Multi-tenant from the first commit, because the product had to serve single-location shops and multi-location chains on the same codebase.

PHP / LaravelVue.jsMySQLStripePayPal

The problem

Repair shops were running intake on paper, inventory in a spreadsheet and payments through a card terminal that knew nothing about either. Parts went missing between locations and no owner could tell which technician or which repair type was actually profitable.

What we shipped

  • Repair ticketing from intake through technician assignment to pickup
  • Inventory tracking with multi-location stock transfers
  • Role-based access so a technician sees jobs and an owner sees margin
  • PayPal and Stripe payments, an embeddable lead-capture widget, and a built-in CMS

Where it stands today

  • Multi-tenant architecture with a subscription admin panel
  • Comparable in scope to RepairDesk, built for a fraction of the incumbent's price
  • Stock transfers between locations are tracked instead of assumed
  • Shops onboard themselves through the subscription flow

Click a stage

One ticket, start to finish.

Intake

Device, fault, customer and quoted price captured at the counter. The customer gets the ticket reference before they leave.

Assign

The job routes to a technician by skill and current load, so the queue is visible instead of living in someone's head.

Parts

Parts consumed pull from local stock. If the branch is out, the transfer request goes to the location that has it.

Repair

Status updates as the technician works. The counter can answer 'is it ready' without walking to the bench.

Pay

Invoice generated from the ticket, paid through Stripe or PayPal, margin recorded against the technician and the repair type.
