Foodies Express

Taiwan's food ordering platform, built by a team eight time zones away.

What it is

A food ordering platform for the Taiwanese market, built as an extension of the client's own operation rather than as an outsourced black box.

PHP / LaravelVue.jsSwiftKotlin

The problem

The client needed development capacity that could plug into an existing operation without disrupting it. The usual failure mode with an external team is that integration cost eats whatever the capacity was worth.

What we shipped

  • Consumer food ordering experience for the Taiwan market
  • Restaurant-side order management
  • Delivery and order status tracking
  • Integration into the client's existing operational workflow

Where it stands today

  • The CEO's own words: we found a way to complement their operations without causing discontinuities on their side
  • Running as Taiwan's food ordering platform
  • The engagement model became a template for later client work
  • Web and mobile surfaces sharing one back end
