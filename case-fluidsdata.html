FluidsData

Drilling fluids data that answers back.

What it is

An AI-powered analytics platform for oil and gas. It turns raw, messy drilling-fluids data into structured dashboards, then layers an agentic assistant on top for predictive guidance.

GolangPythonVue.jsMySQLAgentic AI

The problem

Field and lab data for fluids engineering arrives messy, siloed and slow to interpret. It is the kind of raw technical data that usually means someone spends hours in spreadsheets before a decision gets made, on wells where that delay has a real cost per day.

What we shipped

  • Structured dashboards over raw drilling-fluids data
  • Exploratory analysis tooling for fluids engineers
  • A Fluids Intelligence layer combining domain models with AI
  • An agentic assistant for predictive guidance, not just charts

Where it stands today

  • In active use by teams at Chevron
  • In active use by teams at ExxonMobil
  • In active use by teams at ADNOC
  • Built and maintained for a high bar on data trust
