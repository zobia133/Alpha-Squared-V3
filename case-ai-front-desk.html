AI Front Desk

A receptionist that never sleeps, never forgets, never takes a message wrong.

What it is

An autonomous voice agent that answers real inbound calls around the clock. It understands intent, checks live availability, books the appointment and writes the record into HubSpot. No human in the loop.

VAPITwilioElevenLabsHubSpot CRM

The problem

Staffing a front desk for round-the-clock coverage is expensive and inconsistent, and every missed call is a booking that went to a competitor instead. The client needed calls answered instantly, at any hour, without hiring for it.

What we shipped

  • A voice agent that understands caller intent in real time
  • Live availability checks before offering a time slot
  • Appointment booking with zero human handoff
  • Every call logged straight into HubSpot CRM

Where it stands today

  • Answering real inbound calls, 24 hours a day
  • No manual data entry after a booked call
  • No human required in the loop
  • One of several AI systems we run in production today
